Privacy

Last updated October 19, 2026

perraloans.com is operated by My Bridge Loans Corporation, Fountain Valley, California. This page explains what this website collects and how it is used.

What is collected

If you send the contact form, the site collects your name, email, phone number, the type of loan you are interested in and your message. The form is delivered by Web3Forms, which passes it to us by email.

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics records anonymized visit data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location. Analytics stays off until you choose Allow.

How it is used

Form information is used only to respond to your request about a home loan. We do not sell or rent your information. If you apply for a loan, the information you provide for the application is handled under our separate privacy notice provided with your loan documents, as required by federal law.

Do Not Track

The site does not respond to Do Not Track signals. You can turn analytics off with the No thanks button or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Your California rights

California residents may ask what personal information we have collected through this site and ask us to delete it. Call 949-463-9898 or use the contact form.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
