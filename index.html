Loan programs

As a broker, Jevon compares wholesale lenders for your file instead of fitting you into one bank's menu.

ConventionalPurchase or refinance with standard documentation.
FHAFlexible credit guidelines, popular with first-time buyers.
VAFor eligible veterans, service members and surviving spouses.
JumboLoan amounts above conventional limits.
First-time buyerPrograms and down payment assistance for first homes.
Bank statement (non-QM)For self-employed borrowers whose tax returns understate income.
Cash-out refinanceReplace your mortgage with a larger one and take the difference.
HELOCA line of credit behind your current mortgage.
Reverse mortgageFor homeowners 62 and older, including HECM for Purchase.
Renovation and constructionFHA 203(k), Fannie Mae renovation and construction, bank and private-money programs.

From application to keys

The same four steps whether you are buying, refinancing or taking cash out.

Step 1Apply

Online or by phone. You get a call back the same day with what you qualify for and what documents are needed.

Step 2Review

Your file goes to underwriting early. Buyers can have income, credit and assets reviewed before they write an offer.

Step 3Approval

Credit, income, assets and the property are all signed off, including the appraisal.

Step 4Clear to close

You sign, the loan funds, and you get the keys or the cash.

Questions

Is Perra Home Loans a lender or a broker?

A mortgage broker. Jevon Perra shops your loan to wholesale lenders and picks the one that fits your situation, then runs the file himself from application to closing. Perra Home Loans is a website of My Bridge Loans Corporation.

Where do you lend?

Consumer home loans, including purchase, refinance, HELOC and reverse mortgages, are offered in California only. Jevon is based in Fountain Valley in Orange County.

Who will I actually talk to?

Jevon Perra, the licensed broker. There is no call center and no handoff to a processor you never meet.

Do you do construction loans?

Yes. As a broker, Jevon places construction and renovation loans with lenders that offer them, including FHA 203(k), Fannie Mae renovation and construction programs, bank portfolio programs, and private-money construction loans. Bring the plans and budget and he will match you with the lender that fits.
